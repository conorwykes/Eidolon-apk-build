# Five-star Burst VFX motion study

This composition is a timing and shape study for Eidolon Frontier's six five-star Burst effects. It is not a fullscreen game overlay and is not shipped as video. The runtime implementation follows the same effect identities while anchoring every mark to the fighter, weapon, target, or party-member foot point.

- Format: one 1290 × 710 comparison canvas, six simultaneous 430 × 355 effect studies, six seconds.
- Background: near-black navy only to make additive-looking elemental strokes legible. Runtime backgrounds remain transparent.
- Motion curve: each study grows through anticipation, reaches a readable contact peak, then visibly regresses instead of cutting from 100% to 0%.
- Kael: compact orange ignition around the blade, then a descending cream-hot slash and small embers.
- Lyra: cyan water rings around the body and two curved tide ribbons travelling to contact.
- Brannock: moss-and-stone hammer trail, ground crack, and short ballistic debris.
- Zephyra: yellow-violet charge at the bow, one clearly travelling charged arrow, then a lightning contact star.
- Solenne: restrained ivory and blush healing rings at party foot anchors; no attack or target strike.
- Nyx: violet scythe charge, crescent sweep, and a narrow collapsing rift.
- No typography, captions, full-screen flashes, cinematic character footage, or decorative UI are part of this motion study.
